# Brian Wagon — Character Spec (p5.js)

## Identity
Disembodied brain (Brian Wagon) in fluid-filled bell jar, inside shallow red wagon. No body. Animated avatar for explainer/technical video (16:9 deliverable, 1:1 reusable rig, 10x10 unit canvas).

## Style
Flat vector 2D. Self-contained, reusable p5.js module (single file).

## Colors
- Brain / fluid tint: `#336699` (blue, fluid more transparent / higher value)
- Wagon: red (match TBD — confirm shade)
- Eyes: white, no outline; black pupils (independent L/R, directed gaze + boil/jitter)
- Bell jar: stylized glass dome with reflections

## Anatomy (1:1, 10x10 units)
- Brain: disembodied, floating
- Bell jar: dome over brain, fluid fill, bubbles (excited state), slosh on motion, stylized reflections
- Wagon: shallow red 2D side view; wheels rotate under motion
- Eyes: independent pupils only; directed + boiling
- Mouth: invisible at rest; appears for emotion (not lip-sync)

## Emotions (8)
Happy, Sad, Confused, Excited, Thinking, Celebrating, Fearful, Sleepy — plus emoji characters.

## Rig
Single-file modular p5.js. Reusable. Frame-rate and module exports TBD.
